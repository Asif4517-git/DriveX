- **Racing Games source**: https://github.com/monon98/racing-games, commit e22de885f0f7f305655d148738e033fad345e5a1, copyright 2026 MONON98, MIT. The source has been adapted; retain the full license in `licenses/RACING_GAMES_MIT.txt`.
- **Vehicle model and palette**: `public/models/vehicle.glb` and `public/models/Textures/colormap.png`, from Kenney's [Starter Kit Racing](https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing), `models/vehicle-truck-yellow.glb` and `models/Textures/colormap.png`. Kenney states its included 3D models are CC0; see the [upstream license section](https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing#license) and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Redistribution and modification permitted; attribution not required but appreciated. Model transforms and material tint are adapted at runtime. It is a stylized asset, not a photorealistic branded car.

- Portfolio and Instagram icons in the interface are inline vector artwork; links identify the developer without claiming sole authorship of upstream code or assets.

Original license notice: `licenses/CAR_CONCEPT_LICENSE.md`. No endorsement by authors is implied. Original Kenney CC0 truck remains bundled for provenance but is not the main car.
